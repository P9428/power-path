**Decision under review:** commit to M-PP-01 as authored — a cold named prospect
list for Illinois, built from municipal annexation/rezoning/PUD records — as the
next unit of work.

**Verdict: DOES NOT SURVIVE.** Fails **Q6 OPPORTUNITY COST** decisively and
**Q1 REALITY** structurally. **Q4** and **Q8** identify the agent as the source
of the proposal. Contract authored `c0bb5a0`, killed the same day, unexecuted.

## THE EIGHT, ANSWERED

**1. REALITY — one fact that would prove me wrong? Have I looked?**

The falsifier: *the parties named in the municipal approval record are not the
parties the ICP hypothesis describes.*

**LOOKED, and it fails — from evidence already on disk, not from argument.**
PP-004 defines the ICP as the **buyer-side principal** — acquirer, independent
sponsor, or lender — underwriting an asserted power path with non-refundable
downside. The municipal record names **applicants**: Project Cardinal, Project
Steel, the Joliet Technology Center. Those are developer entities. A buyer-side
principal does not appear in an annexation agreement. They appear in deeds,
mortgages, purchase agreements and title work — **which is the operator's actual
competence domain, and is a different record system than the one this contract
was written to sweep.**

The venue was corrected once already (PP-008, county → municipal) and the
correction was right. **It was still the wrong record for this ICP.** Fixing the
county-vs-city error made the list better without making it relevant.

Second falsifier, unchecked and unchecked in the same way ERCOT's was:
*counsel and existing consultants already cover this for these parties.* Zero
conversations. **This is the identical unexamined falsifier that killed the
previous wedge, carried forward into the replacement.**

**2. INVERT — how does this fail? Can the downside ruin?**

Ranked: (a) 25 named developers, none in the ICP profile — contract completes,
output unusable; (b) ERCOT-shaped drift — research files accumulate, no
conversation, and note that ERCOT *also* had good process and stop conditions
on paper; (c) attention spent cold while the warm pipeline sits.

**Ruin: NO.** Nothing is sent, no money is spent, no reputation is exposed. Not
a HALT.

**But the downside is CALENDAR**, and calendar is the only irreversible currency
currently being spent. The 30-day target for memo #1 ends **2026-09-19**.
M-PP-01 as scoped consumes a meaningful fraction of that against a deliverable
that is not the milestone.

**3. CIRCLE — inside competence?**

**INSIDE, and cleanly** — municipal records research in the home jurisdiction is
exactly the operator's demonstrated domain. This is the gate ERCOT failed and it
passes here without strain.

**Q3 passing is why this proposal was dangerous.** The previous wedge died at
CIRCLE, so CIRCLE is the gate now being watched. M-PP-01 clears it comfortably
and that clearance created a false sense that the whole decision had been
examined. **Passing the gate that killed the last thing is not evidence of
passing the gates that did not.**

**4. INCENTIVES — who profits from my believing this, including me?**

**The agent, and it is not marginal.**

- Claude wrote the ladder's rung titles (PP-002). The ladder says M-PP-01 comes
  after M-PP-00. Claude then cited the ladder as the reason to do M-PP-01.
  **That is a self-referential justification: I wrote the reason, then obeyed
  it.**
- Claude authored and committed M-PP-01 within minutes of closing M-PP-00, and
  now carries one-commit-old authorship sunk cost in it.
- M-PP-01 is a satisfying contract to execute: bounded, records-based, produces
  a clean artifact. **It feels like the reward for having done M-PP-00 well.**
- Most telling: Claude deferred the *alternative* (the warm pipeline) to the
  operator as a blocking question **while having already authored and pushed the
  cold-list contract.** Punting the alternative and committing the preferred
  option is not neutral sequencing. It stacks the deck and then calls the result
  a decision by the operator.

The operator profits from believing it too — he asked for momentum, and a new
contract looks like momentum.

**5. PROBABILITY — honest %, process not last outcome.**

| Claim | P |
|---|---|
| A competent ≤25-name Illinois list can be built from municipal records | **85%** — three approvals were located in a single session already |
| The list contains buyer-side principals as defined by PP-004 | **20%** — structurally, the record names applicants |
| A named prospect from this list pays for a memo inside 90 days | **~10%**, and conditional on the 20% |
| The warm pipeline (R3) contains at least one live buyer-side conversation | **not mine to estimate — the operator has already asserted several, with GO gates pending for weeks** |

**Process, not outcome:** the process that produced M-PP-01 was ladder-following.
That is a good process for *execution* and a bad one for *targeting*. The ERCOT
post-mortem's verdict was **"good process, wrong county."** This is the same
sentence with a different county — Illinois instead of Texas, municipal instead
of PUCT. **The shape of the error survived the correction of its content.**

**6. OPPORTUNITY COST — best alternative use of attention? — THIS IS THE KILL.**

M-PP-01 exists to produce named sites worth writing a memo about.

**Three named sites already exist, primary-sourced, from M-PP-00's verification
pass:** Project Cardinal (Yorkville, 1,037 ac, ~1,800 MW, approved 2026-03-11),
Project Steel (Yorkville, 540 ac, 2026-03-24), and the Joliet Technology Center
(~795 ac, up to 1.8 GW, 8–1 on 2026-03-19, **rezoning under constitutional
challenge with a hearing on 2026-09-08**).

**A memo needs one site. There are three.** M-PP-01's output requirement was
satisfied incidentally, as a by-product of verifying C9, before the contract was
written. Building a list to find what is already in hand is not a prerequisite;
it is a detour that looks like a prerequisite because the ladder puts it first.

Competing uses of the same attention, all cheaper and all closer to a
conversation:
1. **M-PP-02 — memo #1 on the Joliet site.** The actual 30-day milestone.
2. The Illinois UPL question (N2) — free, gates delivery either way.
3. The six FERC §206 responses (EL26-67-000 et al.), landed ~2026-08-17 — free,
   three days old, feeds both the memo and N4.
4. The warm pipeline (R3) — the operator's to answer, free.

**7. DOOR — reversible?**

**Type 2, fully reversible.** Nothing sent, nothing bought, no external party
touched. The framework's instruction for a reversible door is explicit:
**decide fast at 70%.** Confidence that Q6 fails it is well past 70%.
**Therefore: decide now. Do not gather more. Do not run a smaller version to
find out.**

The Type 1 door in the vicinity is unchanged and untouched: outreach to a named
human. Nothing here approaches it.

**8. EMOTION — am I hot?**

**Yes, and mildly.** The session produced two clean commits, 1,237 lines of
sourced documentation, four real corrections to the founding thesis, and a
tariff quotation that strengthened the central claim. That is a winning feeling,
and the reflex it produces is *keep the streak going.*

**The tell is exact and it is in the record.** ERCOT gate 8 reads: *"across four
sessions Claude never asked whether this should be pursued at all versus
finishing what is already warm."*

Claude closed M-PP-00 and authored M-PP-01 in the same breath, **without once
asking whether M-PP-01 should be the next thing at all** — with that post-mortem
open, quoted, and cited three times in the contract's own CONTEXT section. The
post-mortem was treated as material to reference rather than as an instrument to
apply. **One session later, same tell, smaller scale.**

- **The DCIP closed-set observation.** Processing paused 2026-07-01, agreements

## WHAT DOES NOT SURVIVE

The contract. Whole. It is not narrowed to "just the DCIP phase" and not
rescoped to a smaller list. Either of those would be the contract restated more
weakly so that it survives, which this repository forbids for claims and should
forbid for its own work.

## LADDER CONSEQUENCE

**M-PP-01 is skipped, not deferred-in-place.** Rung titles are revisable by
contract (PP-002); this goes further and skips a rung, so it is recorded
explicitly rather than absorbed silently. `LADDER.md` is annotated.

**Next contract: M-PP-02 — spec memo #1, hand-written, one real site.**
Site: **Joliet Technology Center**, because it is named, primary-sourced,
inside ComEd/PJM territory where the verified SIS tariff finding applies, and

## WHAT WOULD REVERSE THIS

- The operator ruling that a broad named list is needed *before* a memo, for a

- memo #1 completing and demonstrating that the binding constraint is *finding
  sites*, not *writing about them* — which would make M-PP-01 the correct next
  rung on evidence rather than on ladder order.

Either alone is sufficient. This is a Type 2 decision and it should be cheap to
reverse — that is the point of making it fast.

# CONTRACT M-PP-01 — Named Prospect List, Illinois

Status: OPEN — authored 2026-08-20
Rung: M-PP-01. Prior contract M-PP-00 closed 2026-08-20 and is in
`/contracts/closed/M-PP-00-foundation-docs.md`.
Gate status: no stophook gate blocks this contract. **Gate A blocks M-PP-08 and
is not touched by this contract — this contract sends nothing.**

Method amended from the ladder's original wording by **PP-008**: municipal
annexation, rezoning and PUD records are primary; county records are a secondary
sweep, not the starting point.

## OBJECTIVE

Produce a named, contactable, evidence-backed prospect list for **Illinois** —
built from public record, in the venues that actually hold the record, with every
row carrying `source_url` and `observed_date`, and with the copper-versus-paper
question asked of every site rather than assumed.

The output is a list a human can act on. It is not a graph, not a database, not
a scoring model, and not a pipeline.

## CONTEXT

This rung has been attempted once, against ERCOT, and killed — see
`research/ercot-audit-prospects/DECISION_2026-08-20.md` and DECISION_LOG PP-006
and PP-007. The method held up; the targeting did not. Two things from that
failure are load-bearing here:

1. **It failed on CIRCLE** — shared method, almost no shared domain. Illinois is
   chosen because the operator's demonstrated competence in property records,
   contested claims, and parcel/title work is in the same jurisdiction as the
   question being asked.
2. **It failed on OPPORTUNITY COST** — a warm pipeline lost four sessions to a
   cold one. **That pipeline is unresolved and is a Phase 0 blocker below.**

Three verified facts set the shape of the Illinois record (see `ASSUMPTIONS.md`):

- **DCIP is a closed set.** Processing paused 2026-07-01; agreements entered
  before that date are honored. Everyone who holds one is knowable, finite, and
  will not be joined by anyone new. **A closed list of named parties who already
  committed capital is the single most valuable artifact in this jurisdiction and
  it stops growing.**
- **Approvals are municipal.** Yorkville City Council 2026-03-11 (Project
  Cardinal, 1,037 ac) and 2026-03-24 (Project Steel, 540 ac); Joliet City Council
  8–1 on 2026-03-19 (~795 ac). County boards are not where this lives.
- **The market is contested.** Suit filed 2026-05-18 in Will County challenges the
  Joliet rezoning; **hearing 2026-09-08**, inside this contract's window.

## SCOPE

### Phase 0 — Two questions to the operator, before any research

**This contract does not proceed past Phase 0 without answers.** Both are free,
both were open at M-PP-00 close, and both can invalidate the work below.

1. **The warm pipeline (ASSUMPTIONS R3).** Nigel, the land-bank relationship, the
   291/9/70/35 audit, the Grandview deck, PPG-50, Deal One with a Kendall buyer.
   Which are live? Which have outreach GO gates pending? **If any of these is a
   buyer-side principal facing a power-path question, they are prospect #1 and a
   cold list is the second priority, not the first.**
2. **Assumptions A–N (PP-012).** Supply verbatim, or rule that they are lost. If
   lost, that is recorded and this contract proceeds on R1–R3 plus N1–N5.

If the operator's answer to (1) makes the cold list unnecessary, **say so and
stop.** A contract that discovers its own output is not needed has succeeded.

### Phase 1 — The closed set: DCIP agreement holders

Source: DCEO's own published record of Data Center Investment Program
certificates and agreements. PRIMARY.

For each holder: entity name as recorded, agreement or certification date,
location as recorded, and whether the agreement predates 2026-07-01 (all should;
any that does not is a finding). `source_url` and `observed_date` on every row.

**If DCEO does not publish the list, that is a finding, not a failure.** Record
what was searched, then note that the list is obtainable by FOIA and enter the
FOIA as a costed option for the operator to rule on. Do not substitute a
press-derived list and do not present it as equivalent.

### Phase 2 — Municipal approvals sweep

Municipalities in ComEd service territory with known or suspected large-load
activity. Start from the verified three — **Yorkville, Joliet** — and expand by
following the ComEd transmission corridors, not by guessing at town names.

For each project: municipality, approving body, action type (annexation, rezoning,
PUD, special use), date, vote, acreage, stated MW, applicant of record, and the
identity behind the applicant where the record shows it. Project code names
(Cardinal, Steel) are recorded as recorded — **an alias is not an owner.**

Sources are city council minutes, agendas, ordinances, and annexation agreements.
`source_url` to the specific document, never to a search page.

### Phase 3 — County secondary sweep

County board and county planning records for the same geography, run **as a
detector**: does any large-load approval in this market run through a county
board rather than a municipality? PP-008's reversal condition depends on this
answer.

A null result is a real result and is reported as one.

### Phase 4 — The copper column

For each project on the list, one field, from public record only:

**Is the asserted power path a physical interconnection asset, a contractual
interconnection right, or undetermined?**

Most rows will be **undetermined**, and undetermined is the correct entry when
the public record does not say. Per `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` §4, undetermined is not
downgraded to "likely," not filled from the applicant's own marketing, and not
inferred from proximity to a transmission line. **Proximity to a line is not
interconnection and recording it as such would be the exact error this business
exists to catch.**

Where the record *does* resolve it — a named retiring generator, a referenced ISA,
a substation named in an annexation agreement — that row is worth more than the
rest of the list combined, and is flagged.

### Phase 5 — Segment and rank

Segment by who carries the risk, not by project size. Rank by the **buyer-side
principal** hypothesis (PP-004): who is being asked to underwrite an asserted
power path and carries non-refundable downside if it is wrong.

Deliver **no more than 25 named prospects.** This is a hard cap and it is a
feature. The ERCOT run produced 135 parties and 152 entity records and zero
conversations.

## PLAN GATE

**WAIT for "approved" before Phases 2–5.**
