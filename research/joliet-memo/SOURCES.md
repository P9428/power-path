## Access notes

`joliet.gov` returns **HTTP 403** to both WebFetch and a normal browser
user-agent via curl. The staff memo (S1) was obtained only because a news
organization rehosted it. **The city's own document portal is, in practice, not
programmatically accessible from this machine** — which is a finding about method
as much as about this site, and is recorded in `GAPS.md` as G6.
